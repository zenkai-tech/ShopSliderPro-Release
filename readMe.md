# ShopSlider Pro

**Offline digital signage for retail shop windows.**
Built by [Zenkai Technologies](https://zenkai-tech.com).

ShopSlider Pro turns any Windows PC and monitor into fully offline digital
signage — images, video, text, and grid collages, managed from one
dashboard. No cloud account, no ongoing internet dependency, no monthly
subscription required to keep the lights on.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-screen dashboard** — manage every display from one place, assign
  monitors, and play them all at once
- **Auto-start on boot** — screens come back to life on their own after a
  power cut or restart, with no one needing to click anything
- **Image, video, text & grid slides** with 9 transitions (fade, slide,
  zoom, wipe, flip & more)
- **Automatic recovery** — missing or corrupted media shows a placeholder
  instead of freezing playback, and the slideshow keeps going
- **100% offline** — works entirely without an internet connection once
  installed and activated
- **Offline, machine-locked licensing** — RSA-signed license keys, no
  forced cloud account, no recurring "phone home" requirement
